03 / The ritual

Forty five minutes, five movements.

  1. 00:00

    The consult

    Coffee or zobo, then a proper conversation about how you wear your hair, day to day.

  2. 05:00

    The shape

    We set your guide lines and build the silhouette from the top down.

  3. 20:00

    The blend

    The part most studios rush. We take our time until the fade disappears into itself.

  4. 35:00

    The line

    Hot lather and a straight razor for the edges: forehead, temple, nape.

  5. 42:00

    The reveal

    Hot towel, cool tonic, the two mirror check. You only leave when you love it.
