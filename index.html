#4 Ritual

Forty-five minutes.
Five acts.

  1. 00:00

    The consult

    Coffee or zobo. We read your crown, your growth pattern and your cut code before a single clipper comes out.

  2. 05:00

    The map

    Guide lines get set at the height you chose. We work from the top down, guard by guard.

  3. 15:00

    The blend

    Lever, flick, repeat. The bit most shops rush is where we spend most of the time.

  4. 35:00

    The line

    Straight razor, hot lather, one breath per stroke. Forehead, temple, nape.

  5. 42:00

    The reveal

    Hot towel, cool tonic, the two-mirror check. You don't pay until you love it.
